# Attribution

Reviewed: 2026-10-30. Based on visible attribution navigation, report
controls, and public help. No account-specific results are included.

## Purpose and prerequisites

Attribution helps you understand how marketing interactions contribute to
recorded conversions. Start with tracking and a meaningful conversion goal.
Paid-ad analysis also needs the relevant ad connections and campaign
tracking described in their setup guides.

An attribution model distributes credit among recorded interactions. A
reporting period selects which activity is examined; the lookback or
look-ahead control limits the surrounding interactions considered. Keep
these choices consistent when comparing results.

## Channel / Source

Use this view for the contribution of marketing channels and individual
sources to a chosen goal.

1. Open **Attribution → Channel / Source**.
2. Select the conversion goal, reporting dates, and available window options.
3. Choose the attribution models offered in the report.
4. Compare channel or source rows and attributed conversion/value measures.
5. Use the available filters or breakdown to focus on the audience of interest.

Example: investigate whether recorded newsletter and search interactions
contribute to a demo-request goal. Different models can assign different
credit to the same recorded conversion; do not add their totals together.

## Paid Ads

Connect the relevant ad network and follow its campaign-tagging instructions.
Open **Attribution → Paid Ads**, select a conversion goal and dates, then
review network and campaign performance. Drill into ad groups or ads where
the connected source provides them.

The report offers two time perspectives:

- **By Conversion Date:** Examine conversions in the selected period and
  preceding eligible touchpoints within the lookback window.
- **By Spend Date:** Examine spend-period activity and conversions within
  the selected look-ahead window.

Review spend, clicks, attributed outcomes, and value together. Revenue-based
measures need recorded conversion values. Recent spend may not yet have a
complete follow-up period, so use comparable windows when evaluating it.

Public reference: [Paid Ads Attribution](https://usermaven.com/docs/articles/paid-ads-attribution-422cb219).

## Content

Use **Attribution → Content** to explore how pages contribute to the selected
goal under different models. Choose the goal and period, compare pages, and
use the visible channel/source filters to narrow the view.

Example: compare product guides and pricing pages for a signup goal.
Attributed contribution and raw page popularity measure different things;
use Web Analytics when the question is simply how often a page was viewed.

## Conversion Paths

Open **Attribution → Conversion Paths**, select the relevant goal and period,
and inspect the recorded sequences associated with conversions. Use it to
understand common marketing paths. For paths around a product event or page,
use the Journey analysis described in [Analytics](analytics.md).

## Days to convert

Open **Attribution → Days to convert**, select the goal and available time
settings, then review the displayed time-to-conversion distribution. Use it
to understand whether outcomes typically occur quickly or after a longer
consideration period.

## Interpretation

Attribution describes credit within the selected data, goal, model, and
window. It does not by itself prove that a channel caused an outcome. Missing
tracking, different goal definitions, and different date perspectives can
make reports disagree. Review visible settings before comparing figures.

The public [Attribution collection](https://usermaven.com/docs/c/attribution-c7d3c134)
links the Channel / Source, Paid Ads, and Content guides. Exact model
availability and advanced configuration are outside this introduction.
