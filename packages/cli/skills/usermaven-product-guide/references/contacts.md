# Contacts, leads, and segments

Reviewed: 2026-10-30. General UI workflows only; no contact records included.

## Contacts

Contacts helps you explore audiences and the activity associated with their
profiles. Visitors may be anonymous; users and companies require the
corresponding information to be supplied to Usermaven.

1. Open **Contacts** and choose Visitors, Users, or Companies.
2. Search, filter, or sort using the controls displayed for that list.
3. Adjust visible columns to focus on relevant attributes and activity.
4. Open a profile to review the information and timeline available to your
   role. Not every profile has every attribute.
5. Save a useful audience as a segment when you want to reuse its definition.

Example: find identified users who have recently used a core feature, then
review their activity pattern. An anonymous visitor is not automatically an
identified user; a company groups activity only when company information is
available.

Public reference: [Contacts Hub](https://usermaven.com/docs/c/contacts-hub-099bb49d).

## Segments

A segment is a saved audience defined by attributes or behavior. Its
membership can change as the matching data changes.

1. Start from the appropriate Contacts list and open its filter controls.
2. Choose relevant attributes, event conditions, or visited-page conditions.
3. Combine conditions with the offered AND/OR controls. For behavior, review
   the time and count conditions where available.
4. Apply the filter, inspect the audience, and save it with a clear name.
5. Reuse the segment in compatible analyses and SaaS reports.

Example: a fictional segment could select trial accounts that created a
project during the past week. It needs the corresponding supplied property
and tracked action. Saving a segment defines an audience; it does not add
tracking or send an outreach message.

Public reference: [Segments](https://usermaven.com/docs/articles/segments-bed66ab6).

## Leads

Leads represent potential customers, such as people submitting a demo or
contact form. A product user and a lead describe different business stages.

1. Complete a supported lead-capture workflow, such as mapped form tracking.
2. Open the Leads view in Contacts when available.
3. Use the visible filters and columns to review captured leads and sources.
4. Open a lead profile to inspect the available activity and attributes.
5. Use a segment to group leads by relevant behavior or supplied details.

Example: compare demo enquiries from different marketing sources. Lead
capture requires the identifying information expected by the configured
workflow; anonymous page views alone do not create a qualified lead.

Public reference: [Lead Tracking](https://usermaven.com/docs/articles/lead-tracking-overview-0263a3a1).

## Audience syncs

Where offered, an audience can be synced to a connected destination. Choose
the audience, destination, visible field choices, and schedule in the sync
form; review its summary before saving. Use the displayed run history to
review outcomes. See [Integrations](integrations.md) for the distinction
between receiving data and sending an audience to another tool.

Explain these features using fictional audience definitions. Do not request
or include actual contact lists, email addresses, profile screenshots, or
exports in the public guide.
