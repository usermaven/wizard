# Maven AI

Reviewed: 2026-10-30. General instructions from visible AI screens and
public help. This reference contains no workspace conversations or results.

## Ask questions

Maven AI is the assistant inside Usermaven for exploring workspace analytics
in plain language. Open **Maven AI**, start a conversation, and describe the
question, time period, audience, and business outcome. Review the answer's
charts and definitions, then ask a focused follow-up if needed.

Example: “Compare weekly project creation over the past month for trial and
paid users.” This requires the corresponding tracked action and audience
property. The question should use the workspace's actual business definition.

Maven AI can help create charts, analyses, and dashboards where supported.
Review the generated configuration and result rather than assuming the
assistant inferred the intended event or audience correctly.

## Agents and Insights

Agents provides scheduled investigations. Create an agent from a template or
a stated objective, review its scope and business definitions, choose when
it should run, and select the delivery options shown. Review the draft
before enabling it. Use Insights and run reports to inspect findings; pause
or resume future checks using the available controls.

Reference: [Maven AI](https://usermaven.com/docs/articles/maven-ai-talk-to-your-data-5b9878f7).

## Working with the result

Give enough context to distinguish a traffic question from a conversion,
engagement, or retention question. For generated analyses, check the selected
goal, events, audience level, period, and filters before interpreting the
numbers. Findings suggest what to investigate; they do not establish causes
by themselves.

For scheduled investigations, the form exposes an objective, scope,
definitions, schedule/timezone, and update preferences. These are business
instructions; no knowledge of internal AI execution is required.

## Sharing and connected tools

Conversation and result sharing controls are offered where supported. Review
who should see the content before using them. External AI connections are
managed through the app's **AI & MCP** area; see [Integrations](integrations.md).

Installing this product-guide skill in Claude or an OpenAI product provides
general Usermaven knowledge. It does not itself connect that assistant to
Usermaven or retrieve live metrics. Do not claim account access unless a
separately authorized connection is actually available.
