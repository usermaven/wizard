# Reports and alerts

Reviewed: 2026-10-30. Based on visible report forms and public help.

## Reports

Reports delivers recurring summaries so a team can review performance
without opening each analysis manually.

1. Open **Reports** and choose **Create Report**.
2. Give it a descriptive name and select an available report type.
3. Choose the dashboard, saved analysis, conversion goal, or other context
   requested by that type.
4. Set the reporting period and comparison options offered.
5. Choose a daily, weekly, or monthly schedule and review the delivery time
   and displayed timezone.
6. Select the available delivery channel and intended recipients.
7. Review the summary and create the report.

Example: schedule a weekly acquisition summary for the team responsible for
marketing. The delivery schedule and the data period are separate choices.

From the report list, review schedules and use the offered controls to edit,
pause, resume, or delete a configuration. **Send now** is available where
supported. A scheduled report does not replace the underlying dashboard or
analysis.

Reference: [Reports](https://usermaven.com/docs/articles/reports-3a26670f).

## Report types and customization

The current form includes web summaries and contexts for Attribution,
Funnels, Journeys, Trends, Retention, and dashboards where available. Follow
the selected type's fields rather than expecting identical options across
all reports. AI summary settings, language, and period comparisons appear
where supported.

Keep a report's goal, filters, dates, and audience aligned with the source
analysis. If it differs from a dashboard, first compare these visible
settings. Do not request internal delivery logs or message contents to
explain general reporting behavior.

## Alerts

Alerts watch configured conditions rather than simply delivering a summary
on a calendar. Open the **Alerts** area in Reports or the alert control on a
supported analysis.

1. Choose the supported alert type and the item to monitor.
2. Select the metric or step and the condition offered by the form.
3. Set a threshold and the available check frequency.
4. Choose notification channels and recipients.
5. Review and save, then manage it from the alerts list.

Examples include a funnel completion-rate condition, a change in a trend
metric, or conversion-goal activity. Email and Slack options depend on the
alert and available setup. An alert indicates that its configured condition
was met; it does not explain the underlying cause.

Reference: [Alerts](https://usermaven.com/docs/articles/alerts-1d674321).

The public guide contains no actual recipients, report results, or channel
details. Describing delivery does not authorize sending or scheduling it.
