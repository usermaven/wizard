# Settings and data health

Reviewed: 2026-10-30. A general orientation to visible settings only. This
guide omits security procedures, technical diagnostics, and internal checks.

## Workspace settings

Open the workspace's Settings control and choose the relevant section.
Review existing visible selections, change only the intended preference,
and use the form's save control. Available sections depend on your access.

| Section | General purpose and basic use |
| --- | --- |
| Setup | Return to the platform-specific tracking instructions and setup guidance |
| General | Review workspace details, domains, timezone, and the workspace choices shown |
| Engagement Criteria | Review the activity criteria used by the available engagement experience |
| Team Members | Invite or manage teammates and choose appropriate access through the visible form |
| Pixel white-labelling | Follow the offered setup for a branded tracking domain; technical configuration belongs to its dedicated guide |
| Customize Menu | Show or hide supported navigation items for the workspace experience |
| Channel Mapping | Review or define how matching traffic is grouped into marketing channels |
| Integrations | Connect supported data services; see [Integrations](integrations.md) |
| AI & MCP | Manage the supported AI connection options offered by the app |
| Reporting | Choose defaults such as reporting period, currency, display preferences, and available AI-summary settings |
| Exclude Traffic | Review the visible choices for excluding unwanted traffic from reporting |
| Activity Log | View the workspace change history available to your role |

Example: choose the business's reporting timezone and currency so team
members interpret reports consistently. Hiding a navigation item changes
the menu experience; do not treat it as a statement about another person's
access. Use the actual team-access controls for that purpose.

Public reference: [Workspace Settings](https://usermaven.com/docs/c/workspace-settings-94a1f5e1).

## Account settings

The account menu provides profile/general information, Security, and
applicable organization options. Plan & Billing shows the subscription and
usage information available to your role. White Label offers the branding
choices available to the organization. Refer & Earn and support-access
preferences may also appear.

For the visible White Label tabs and basic field purposes, read
[White Label](white-label.md): Branding & Theme, Custom Domains, and Email
Configuration. It covers general usage without saved customer values or
provider credentials.

Open the relevant page and follow its visible form. This guide gives no
credential-management steps, billing prices, account identifiers, private
contact information, or privileged procedures. For those account-specific
questions, use the app's own guidance or support.

## Data Health / Measurement Trust Center

The navigation's **Data Health** control opens the **Measurement Trust
Center** in the current UI. It provides a visible measurement-health summary
and recommendations for the workspace.

Open the page, read its summary and displayed recommendations, and follow
the user-facing guidance relevant to your setup. A health summary helps
orient a review; it is not a guarantee that every business metric is correct.

This guide deliberately excludes individual diagnostic checks, evidence
payloads, scoring rules, internal recovery operations, and backend details.
For issues beyond the visible guidance, use in-app support without sharing
secrets or raw logs in a general product-help conversation.
